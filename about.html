THE GENESIS

Maison Yojaki was founded with a singular conviction: luxury Indian perfumery should command the global stage alongside Grasse, Paris, and Milan.

From sacred blue lotus blossoms collected at dawn to tempestuous ambergris accords aged in dark cellar barrels, each composition is an unhurried devotion to olfactory permanence.

TECHNICAL DISTINCTION

Why Cold Maceration Defines Yojaki

Mass commercial perfumery heats essence oils to artificially speed compounding. At Maison Yojaki, we refuse to compromise.

01

28% Pure Extrait

We compound at Extrait de Parfum concentration (28%), far above typical Eau de Toilette (8-12%) or commercial Eau de Parfum (15%).

02

60 Days Cold Rest

Bottled oils rest undisturbed in chilled dark steel chambers at 12°C for two complete lunar cycles, allowing top and base molecules to interlock naturally.

03

Hand-Crimped Seal

Every French glass bottle is crimped by hand at our Ashok Vihar atelier with a vacuum gold collar and verified under magnifying optics.
