✦ ARTISANAL SCIENCE ✦

Why Cold Maceration Defines Yojaki

Mass commercial perfumery heats essence oils to artificially speed compounding. At Maison Yojaki, we refuse to compromise.

01 EXTRAIT GRADE

28% Pure Extrait

We compound at true Extrait de Parfum concentration (28%), far above commercial Eau de Parfum (12–15%) or typical Eau de Toilette (8–10%). This extreme oil density produces remarkable longevity exceeding 14 uninterrupted hours on skin and linen.

02 60-DAY MATURATION

60 Days Cold Rest

Bottled oils rest undisturbed in chilled dark steel chambers at 12°C across two complete lunar cycles. Top citrus notes and dense base resins seamlessly integrate without thermal degradation or chemical accelerators.

03 PARISIAN CRIMPING

Hand-Crimped Seal

Every French glass flacon is crimped by hand at our Ashok Vihar atelier with a vacuum gold collar, hot wax-stamped with our golden monogram crest, and verified under optical magnification before entering the velvet presentation coffret.
