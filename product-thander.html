THE 60-DAY COLD MACERATION PROCESS

Unlike mass commercial perfumery, THANDER is never heat-accelerated. The pure botanical extrait matures for 60 uninterrupted days in temperature-regulated sealed vessels in North West Delhi, India, creating a seamless, creamy integration between the violent sea spray and dense resinous woods.

HOW TO APPLY FOR 14+ HOUR PROJECTION

Extrait de Parfum possesses high oil density (28%). Mist directly onto clean, hydrated skin at the pulse points: clavicle, wrists, and behind the ears. Do not rub wrists together, which fractures the volatile top sea-ozone molecules.
